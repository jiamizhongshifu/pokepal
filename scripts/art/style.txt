ART STYLE (apply strictly; it must match the attached style reference image):
- Flat vector illustration like a polished modern mobile learning-app campaign.
- Shapes are geometric but soft: rounded trapezoids, ovals, slanted rounded rectangles and asymmetric blobs; slightly angular, never perfectly symmetric.
- Absolutely NO outlines or line art. Shapes are defined only by fill color.
- Each color has one darker flat shade shape with a hard edge (cel shading) and occasionally one lighter highlight shape. No gradients, no texture, no soft airbrush.
- Bright saturated colors.
- Faces: big white eye shapes, often one eye slightly larger than the other, dark pupils pushed toward the direction of action, simple solid brow or eyelid shapes; mouths as solid shapes with a darker inside and a tongue.
- Limbs are chunky rounded capsule shapes (never lines), small rounded hands and feet.
- Lively poses with squash and stretch and slight tilt; strong, instantly readable silhouettes.
- No text, letters, numbers, symbols, logos or watermarks anywhere.
- Characters are ORIGINAL island creatures for a children's (ages 3-6) English-learning app: a real baby animal combined with one nature element. Simple silhouette, two or three main colors, one signature feature that stays recognizable at small sizes.
- They must not resemble any existing Pokémon species, owls, Duolingo characters, Sanrio, Molang or any other franchise character.
